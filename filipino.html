Tips for better pronunciation
  • Write words the way they are spoken in the language, with normal spelling. The voice reads letters, so English words may sound off.
  • Write numbers as words, for example "dalawampu" instead of "20".
  • Commas add a short pause and periods a longer one.
  • If a line sounds odd, press Generate again. Each run is slightly different. Lower Variation makes it steadier.
